FAQ

Content Studio FAQ

It depends on the length of your booking, but a typical Quick Content Session ($499, 2 hours) produces one clean recording ready to cut into multiple pieces, while a longer Full Production Day ($1,499, 8 hours) can cover several formats at once.

Both are available. You can take the raw files and edit in-house, or add editing to your booking — several of our bundles include episode edits.

Long-form video, short-form vertical clips, and audio-only cuts can all be captured from the same recording, since every session is filmed on 3 cameras with isolated audio by default.

The studio and equipment are the same either way — a content day is simply booking longer or more frequently so one visit covers several weeks of posting, instead of one piece of content.
