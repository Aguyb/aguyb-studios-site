What is a podcast studio in Jacksonville? AGUYB Studios is a professional podcast and video production studio in Jacksonville, Florida, designed for businesses, creators, entrepreneurs and organizations that need professional multi-camera podcast and video production. The studio includes a 3-camera cinema setup, broadcast-quality microphones on an isolated multi-track soundboard, and full lighting, run by an on-site producer, available by the hour starting at $399.
