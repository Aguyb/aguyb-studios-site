How It Works

How On‑Site Podcast Production Works

01

Book Your Session

Choose your date and location. You’ll need to provide power access, a quiet recording environment, and any required location permissions.

02

Time Coverage

Our team arrives with equipment and begins setup. Please provide 1 hour of access before recording for setup and load‑in.

03

You Record

You focus on the conversation. We manage cameras, lighting, audio and production throughout the session.

04

File Delivery Within 72 Hours

Please provide 1 hour of access after recording for teardown and load‑out. Files are delivered securely within 72 hours.
