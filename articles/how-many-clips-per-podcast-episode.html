How Many Clips Should You Get From One Podcast Episode?

A practical breakdown of how we turn a single recording session into a month of short-form content.

A single hour-long podcast recording contains far more content than most people expect, in practice, most of our clients walk away with somewhere between eight and fifteen usable short clips from a single episode, depending on how much genuinely quotable material came up in the conversation. That is enough to post two to three times a week for a full month without recording again.

The highest-performing clips are rarely the planned talking points, they are the unscripted moments, a strong opinion stated plainly, a specific number or result, a short story with a clear beginning and end. Editors comb the full recording specifically for those thirty-to-ninety-second moments rather than chopping the episode into equal segments.

Different lengths suit different platforms, and one long session naturally produces all of them. Fifteen-second vertical clips work for Reels and TikTok, sixty-to-ninety-second clips work for LinkedIn and YouTube Shorts, and a handful of three-to-five-minute segments can stand alone as their own posts for viewers who want more context than a quick clip gives.

The practical upside is cadence without constant production. Recording one episode a month but posting from it several times a week keeps a consistent presence without needing a camera crew every few days, the recording is the expensive, occasional step, the clipping is the repeatable, cheap one.

If you are sitting on old recordings that were only ever published as full episodes, they are very likely still full of unclipped short-form content. Send us a link and we can tell you roughly how many clips are in there before you record anything new.
