How to Start a Podcast in Jacksonville, FL (2025 Guide)

Thinking about starting a podcast in Jacksonville, FL? AGUYB Studios breaks down everything you need to know — from choosing the right equipment and finding your niche to recording your first episode in a professional studio. This 2025 guide is your step-by-step roadmap to launching a podcast that sounds great and grows your audience.

Jacksonville Has 64,000 Podcast Listeners — and Almost No Local Shows

That gap is the opportunity. Starting a podcast in Jacksonville right now — done at a high level — is one of the smartest brand and business moves you can make.

Jacksonville Is a Media-Undersaturated City — and That’s Rare

Jacksonville is one of the largest cities in the United States by land area and population, with over 900,000 people. Yet, it has no dominant local podcast ecosystem.

Compared to cities like Miami, Atlanta, or New York, the local media landscape is surprisingly thin.

That’s not a weakness — it’s an opportunity.

If you start a podcast in Jacksonville and do it right, you’re not joining a conversation. You’re starting one.

Over 64,000 people in Jacksonville already listen to podcasts regularly. Nearly half of them (47.6%) are between 18 and 24, and another 35% are between 25 and 34.

This is a young, active, and business-driven audience.

The businesses exist. The entrepreneurs exist. The stories exist.

The only thing missing is the platform.

Podcasting Isn’t Emerging — It’s Dominant

Podcasting has already become one of the most powerful content channels available today.

Over 158 million Americans listen to podcasts monthly • More than 70% of listeners finish episodes • Podcast audiences are typically higher income and more educated • 54% of listeners are between 18–34 • The global podcast market is worth over $39 billion

This means: deep attention, high trust, strong influence. Unlike social media, where content is quickly skipped, podcasts create real engagement.

The Jacksonville Advantage

Jacksonville offers a unique combination of factors that make it a powerful podcast market.

Low Competition, Fast Authority

In bigger cities, you compete for attention. In Jacksonville, you can become the voice in your niche much faster.

Growing Business Ecosystem

The city is expanding in real estate, logistics, hospitality, and tech. Businesses need exposure and storytelling.

A podcast becomes a networking tool, a client acquisition system, and a branding platform.

Untold Cultural Stories

Jacksonville has strong talent and entrepreneurial energy, but very little media documenting it. This is where a podcast creates impact.

What a High-Level Podcast Looks Like Today

Podcasting is no longer just audio. The podcasts that win today are video-first, multi-platform (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram), high production quality, and strongly branded.

One recording session can produce a full YouTube episode, 10–30 short clips, and audio for Spotify and Apple Podcasts.

Production quality matters. It directly affects how your audience and potential guests perceive your brand.

How to Start a Podcast (Step-by-Step)

1. Define Your Positioning

Choose a niche: business, real estate, culture, or entrepreneurship. The goal is to own a space, not speak to everyone.

2. Build a Content Strategy

Each episode should generate one long-form video, multiple short clips, and written or social content. If you’re not repurposing content, you’re losing value.

3. Invest in Production Quality

A serious podcast includes multiple cameras, professional lighting, high-quality audio, and a branded set. First impressions matter.

4. Distribute Across Platforms

YouTube for long-form, Instagram and TikTok for growth, Spotify and Apple for loyal listeners. Each platform plays a role.

5. Stay Consistent (This Is Everything)

Consistency is what builds audience over time. Most podcasts fail not because of bad ideas, but because they stop.

Why Doing Everything Yourself Doesn’t Work

Many creators quit because they underestimate production. When you try to do everything, you lose time, you lose consistency, you lose focus, and most importantly, you lose opportunities. Instead of booking guests, growing your network, and building your brand, you’re stuck editing videos.

The Smart Move: Outsource Production

Working with a production company like AGUYB STUDIOS allows you to focus on what matters most: your voice and your network.

Professional production handles studio setup (cameras, lighting, audio), full editing (video and audio), short-form content creation, branding (thumbnails, visuals), distribution across platforms, and consistent turnaround.

This allows you to stay consistent, scale faster, and position yourself as premium.

The Opportunity Is Now

Jacksonville is still early in podcast culture. The audience exists. The demand exists. The opportunity exists. What’s missing is the creator who shows up consistently with quality.

That creator doesn’t just build a podcast. They build a media brand, a powerful network, and a long-term business asset.

Starting a podcast in Jacksonville isn’t just about content. It’s about positioning yourself at the center of a growing city’s voice.

The question is simple: are you going to watch it happen — or lead it?
